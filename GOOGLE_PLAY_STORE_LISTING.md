## Long Description:

**Track Your Emotions, Grow Your Inner Garden**

Positive Emotional Tree is a gentle, beautiful mood tracking app that helps you observe your emotions without judgment. Instead of writing long journal entries, simply choose a fruit that matches how you feel each day and watch your emotional garden grow.

**🌸 Simple & Beautiful Mood Tracking**
Pick from five colorful fruits to represent your daily mood:
• 🍒 Cherry - Passion & Tension (strong emotions, drive, or nervous excitement)
• 🍉 Watermelon - Joy & Holiday (carefree lightness, summer vibes)
• 🍊 Orange - Energy & Optimism (activity, inspiration, movement)
• 🍍 Pineapple - Creativity & Unusualness (unconventional, dreaming, unique)
• 🍇 Grape - Peace & Harmony (balance, inner calm, simple pleasures)

**📅 Calendar View**
Navigate through your emotional journey with an intuitive calendar. Select any day, choose your mood fruit, and see your emotional patterns unfold over time. Your moods create a beautiful visual garden that you can explore and understand better.

**💡 Daily Personalized Tips**
Receive gentle, personalized tips based on your current mood. Each fruit comes with thoughtful suggestions to help you navigate your day with more awareness and kindness toward yourself.

**🎮 Relaxing Plinko Game**
Take a break with our calming Plinko-style relaxation game. Drop colorful balls and watch them bounce through pins - a perfect way to unwind and find your center. No pressure, no competition, just peaceful play.

**✨ Key Features:**
• Beautiful, minimalist design with soft pastel colors
• Quick mood logging - no lengthy writing required
• Visual calendar to track your emotional patterns
• Personalized daily tips based on your mood
• Relaxing Plinko bubble game for stress relief
• Privacy-focused - all data stored locally on your device
• Gentle approach to emotional awareness
• Grow your positive emotional tree over time

**🌳 Why Positive Emotional Tree?**

We created this app to help you:
• Carefully observe your emotions without judgment
• Learn to let go of negativity naturally
• See the beauty in every day - even the simplest ones
• Build emotional awareness through gentle practice
• Create a personal emotional garden you can explore

**Perfect for:**
• Anyone looking for a simple, non-intimidating way to track moods
• People who want to build emotional awareness
• Those seeking a gentle approach to mental wellness
• Users who prefer visual, playful interfaces over text-heavy apps
• Anyone who wants to relax and unwind with a calming game

**Privacy & Data:**
Your emotional data stays on your device. We don't collect, share, or sell your personal information. Your mood journey is yours alone.

Start growing your Positive Emotional Tree today. Every day is a chance to understand yourself better, one fruit at a time.
